MyCell - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Summary

MyCell does not collect, store, sell or share personal data on any server. The author does not operate any backend for the Game.

2. Data stored locally

Your progress (stars, levels, machines, minerals, rebirths, etc.) is saved inside the PowerPoint file on your own device. It never leaves your device through the Game.

3. Discord Rich Presence

If Discord is running locally, the Game sends short activity text (such as your current action, Cell level, rebirth count and galaxy) to your local Discord client through Discord's Rich Presence interface, so it can be shown on your profile. The Game does not read your Discord messages, friends list, email or any other Discord account data, and does not use Discord OAuth. You can turn this off in Discord (Settings → Activity Privacy). Discord's own handling of data is described in Discord's Privacy Policy.

4. Third parties

The Game does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

5. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above will change when it is.

MyCell - Política de Privacidad

Última actualización: 12 de octubre de 2026

MyCell no recopila, almacena, vende ni comparte datos personales en ningún servidor; el autor no opera ningún backend. Tu progreso se guarda dentro del archivo de PowerPoint en tu propio dispositivo.
